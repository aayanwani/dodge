DODGE

born to perform

The American muscle. Founded by Dodge brothers in 1914.

1900: The Dodge Brothers Company opened in Detroit, initially making precision parts and bicycle components.
1903: The brothers partnered with Henry Ford, supplying engines, transmissions, and axles for early Ford cars.
1914: After splitting with Ford over a business dispute, the brothers launched their own complete vehicle, the Dodge Model 30. It featured an innovative all-steel body that revolutionized automotive durability.

1960s–1970s: Dodge cemented its legacy in American culture by producing legendary high-performance muscle cars, including the Dodge Charger and Dodge Challenger.

1980s–1990s: Dodge diversified its lineup with minivans, SUVs, and trucks, while continuing to innovate in performance vehicles. The Dodge Viper, introduced in 1992, became an icon of American sports cars.

2000s–Present: Dodge has embraced modern technology and design, producing

vehicles that combine performance, style, and practicality. The Dodge Challenger and Charger continue to be celebrated for their powerful engines and aggressive styling, while the brand also focuses on sustainability with electric vehicle initiatives.

Dodge Viper

The Dodge Viper is a high-performance American sports car known for its powerful V10 engine and aggressive design.

It has been a symbol of American automotive excellence for decades.

The top speed of a stock Dodge Viper ranges from 177 mph (in high-downforce ACR track models) up to 206–208 mph in standard coupe and GTS configurations.

Dodge Hellcat

The Dodge Hellcat is a high-performance variant of the Dodge Challenger and Charger, known for its supercharged V8 engine producing over 700 horsepower.

It has been a symbol of American automotive excellence for decades.

The top speed of a stock Dodge Hellcat ranges from 199 mph (in high-performance models) up to 204 mph in the Challenger SRT Hellcat Redeye configuration.

In 2021, Dodge introduced the Dodge Charger SRT Hellcat Redeye Widebody, which is one of the fastest and most powerful sedans in the world, with a top speed of around 203 mph.

In 2022, Dodge announced the Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170, a limited-production drag racing car with a top speed of approximately 211 mph, making it one of the fastest production cars in the world.

In 2023, Dodge unveiled the Dodge Charger SRT Super Stock, a high-performance variant of the Charger with a top speed of around 203 mph, designed for drag racing enthusiasts.
