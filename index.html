Heritage

The legacy of American performance.

1900

The Dodge Brothers Company opened in Detroit, beginning with precision parts and bicycle components that set the tone for future craftsmanship.

1903

The brothers partnered with Henry Ford, supplying engines, transmissions, and axles for early Ford vehicles and proving their engineering excellence.

1914

After a split with Ford, the brothers launched their own complete automobile, the Dodge Model 30, featuring an all-steel body that redefined durability and innovation.

1960s–70s

Dodge cemented its place in automotive culture with legendary muscle cars like the Charger and Challenger, turning performance into a national obsession.

1980s–90s

The brand expanded into minivans, SUVs, and trucks while still producing icons such as the Viper, an unmistakable symbol of raw American power.

2000s–Now

Dodge continues to combine modern technology, aggressive styling, and performance-focused engineering with models that remain thrilling and unforgettable.
